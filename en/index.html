About me

Training that stands behind every clinical decision

Welcome, colleague. I am Dra. Bianca Rubí Flores Reyes, an anesthesiologist. I earned my medical degree at the School of Medicine of the Universidad Autónoma de Nayarit (2010–2017), where I built the clinical and human foundations that guide my daily practice.

I completed my residency in Anesthesiology at the Unidad Médica de Alta Especialidad (UMAE) No. 25 del IMSS in Monterrey, Nuevo León, with academic endorsement from the Universidad de Monterrey (UDEM), graduating in 2023. This training prepared me for comprehensive management of the surgical patient: from pre-anesthetic evaluation through postoperative recovery.

As part of my continuing education, I completed a postgraduate certificate in Cardiovascular Anesthesia for Non-Cardiac Surgery and hold certification from the Consejo Mexicano de Anestesiología (Mexican Board of Anesthesiology).

Services

Anesthesia support for your professional practice

Every anesthetic plan is tailored to the type of procedure and the patient's condition. Request and book my availability directly.

🩺

For surgeons

  • General anesthesia — for procedures requiring complete loss of consciousness.
  • Regional anesthesia — techniques adapted to the type of surgery and the patient's profile.
  • Pre-anesthetic evaluation — prior assessment to plan the safest management.
  • Perioperative pain management — pain control before and after surgery.
🦷

For dental surgeons

  • Dental sedation — anesthesia support for oral surgery, complex extractions, or patients requiring special management (anxiety, comorbidities).
🧲

Outside the operating room

  • Sedation for imaging — MRI, CT, and other studies that call for sedation (claustrophobia, required immobility).
  • Sedation outside the OR — diagnostic or therapeutic procedures beyond the surgical suite.
📍 Available at hospitals and private practices throughout Baja California Sur: La Paz, Cabo San Lucas, and San José del Cabo.

Ethics and work philosophy

My commitment to you and your patient

Anesthesiology demands more than technical knowledge: it demands presence, empathy, and absolute responsibility for the patient's life at a moment of utmost vulnerability.

01

Safety above all

Every anesthetic plan is designed individually, placing patient safety above any other consideration, in close coordination with the specialist in charge.

02

Clear, professional communication

Direct, timely coordination with surgeons, dental surgeons, and imaging teams, so every procedure proceeds with the information it requires and without setbacks.

03

Availability and commitment

Punctuality and prompt confirmation are essential when another professional depends on my support. I keep my schedule up to date and respond as quickly as possible.
